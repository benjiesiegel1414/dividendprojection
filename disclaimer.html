The assumptions behind every result

To keep the math transparent, the calculator deliberately simplifies how funds behave. Unless you change a setting, every projection assumes:

  • The share price never changes. In reality, share prices rise and fall, sometimes sharply. Some high-yield and option-income funds have historically lost share value over time, often called price decay or NAV erosion.
  • The yield stays at today's level. The yield shown is a current snapshot. Distributions are not guaranteed and can be reduced, suspended or eliminated at any time.
  • Dividends don't grow unless you enter a growth rate, and any growth rate you enter is applied smoothly and consistently, which real dividend histories rarely are.
  • Payouts arrive on schedule at the fund's stated frequency, in equal amounts, and are reinvested immediately at the same flat share price if reinvesting is turned on. Real reinvestment may involve fractional-share limits, timing differences or fees.
  • Taxes are applied as one flat rate that you choose, with a default of 0%. Real dividend taxation depends on the type of distribution (qualified, ordinary, return of capital), your income, your state and your account type.
  • Deposits are made evenly across each year and grow only by the rate you choose.
  • No trading costs, account fees or inflation are included. A dollar of dividend income in 20 years will likely buy less than a dollar does today.

Fund expense ratios are generally deducted by the fund before it pays distributions, so the yield already reflects them. The Site's estimate of fees on your balance is for illustration only.
