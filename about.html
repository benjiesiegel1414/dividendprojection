Why this exists

Most dividend calculators are either buried behind an account, stuffed with outdated yields, or so complicated you give up before you get an answer. We wanted the opposite: type in what you'd invest, pick a fund, and immediately see what it could pay you each month.

DividendProjection is built by a dividend investor with more than ten years of experience, for other dividend investors. The goal is simple: share genuinely useful tools for free and help more people see how steady, reinvested income can add up over time.

How the calculator works

You enter a starting amount, a monthly deposit, a fund and a number of years. The calculator then steps through every single payout the fund would make, whether that's weekly, monthly or quarterly, adding your deposits, paying out the dividend, taking out tax if you've set a rate, and reinvesting the rest if you've chosen to.

To keep the math honest and easy to follow, it assumes the share price never changes and that dividends stay flat unless you add a growth rate yourself. Real markets won't behave that way, so treat every result as an illustration rather than a forecast. The disclaimer explains every assumption in detail.

Where the data comes from

Fund data, including yields, expense ratios, payout frequency, assets and ratings, comes from the TopDividendETFsPRO.com database, which is reviewed and updated daily. The everyday expense comparisons, like the average phone bill or rent, come from public sources such as the Bureau of Labor Statistics, the Social Security Administration and the U.S. Energy Information Administration, and each one is linked right on the page.
