About SPYI

SPYI was NEOS's first high-income ETF. It holds the stocks in the S&P 500 and sells call options on the S&P 500 index (SPX) to fund its monthly payouts. It may also buy SPX calls at higher strike prices, a call spread that lets the fund keep some upside in rising markets.

Because it uses SPX index options, the option gains and losses fall under Section 1256 and are taxed 60% long-term and 40% short-term. NEOS reports that SPYI's distributions have been classified as return of capital, and the fund also looks for tax loss harvesting opportunities.

Compared with its Nasdaq sibling QQQI, SPYI sits on a broader and less tech-heavy stock portfolio and has a longer track record, while usually paying a little less.

SPYI FAQ

What is SPYI's dividend yield?

SPYI's dividend yield is currently 12.15%, based on the TopDividendETFsPRO database, which is updated daily.

How often does SPYI pay dividends?

SPYI pays monthly.

How much does $10,000 in SPYI pay per month?

At the current yield, about $101 a month on average, or $1,215 a year, assuming the yield stays the same.

How much do I need in SPYI to make $1,000 a month?

About $98,800 at the current yield.

What is SPYI's total return?

SPYI has returned +80% since it launched in 2022, with dividends reinvested, about +15.4% a year on average.

What is the difference between SPYI and QQQI?

SPYI uses the S&P 500 and SPX options, QQQI uses the Nasdaq-100 and NDX options. SPYI is broader and older, QQQI is more tech-heavy and usually yields more.
